# OCTAVE-scripts-to-solve-navigation-problems

Open-source OCTAVE scripts to solve the direct and inverse navigation problems on the WGS84 spheroid.
Based on Karney and Rapp jobs.

Open-source software programs have been developed in OCTAVE to solve the everyday navigation problems on a spheroid. An explanation of is provided, and for a better understanding, a geometric analysis of the sphere, the ellipse and the spheroid is carried out, correctly interpreting and representing three-dimensional space, knowing the objectives and the use of graphic representation, highlighting the fundamental properties that are applied to solve both, the direct and inverse mathematical problem. The work is complemented by two practical studies, modelling situations and solving problems with physic-mathematical techniques, carried out on the spheroid and compared with the results obtained on the sphere using the NAVESF (Arós et al., 2012, pág. 296) software, as well as a comparative study of the gaining resulting from navigation by a spheroid geodesic instead of navigation along a great circle on a sphere.
